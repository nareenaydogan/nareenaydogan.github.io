Walls

RapidWall™ offers a complete building wall solution manufactured with factory-pre-sized studs, reducing on-site labor, excessive workmanship, and overall construction costs.

Its lightweight structure, weighing approximately 5 lbs./sq. ft. (MgO), simplifies installation and reduces the need for heavy construction equipment.

Designed as an advanced structural insulated panel, RapidWall™ combines 16-gauge steel RapidStud™ framing with a high-pressure-injected, high-density, in-mold closed-cell, water-based ecological polyurethane foam core.

With a foam density of 2.2 pcf, the system delivers exceptional insulation performance, achieving a minimum R-28 rating with the 4.5-inch panel. Its heavy-duty 16-gauge steel studs provide structural strength and long-term durability.

Interior MgO facings come paint-ready, eliminating the need for traditional drywall and helping streamline the finishing process.
